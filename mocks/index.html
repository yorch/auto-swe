The shift

The engine was always the product.

auto-swe already runs every job as a configurable, versioned DAG. The pivot makes that engine first-class and ships every domain as an installable pack — a bundle of templates, steps, agent roles, skills, scanners, connection types, and memory schema.

The crowded part — "compose agent steps" — isn't the moat. The moat is everything around it: durability, governance, security, memory, and cost. SWE becomes the flagship proof, not the ceiling.

One bundle. Everything a domain needs.

A pack declares its templates, step definitions, roles, skills, scanner patterns, connection types, triggers, and memory scopes in a single versioned manifest. Installing a pack registers its steps into the engine and seeds its config — idempotently.

SWE is extracted into pack-swe first (P0, zero behavior change), which forces a clean platform/app boundary before the engine refactor begins.
